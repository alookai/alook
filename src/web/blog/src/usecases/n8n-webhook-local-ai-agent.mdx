Automation flows in n8n are great at collecting signals across hundreds of SaaS tools, yet most collected items end up sitting unread in a Notion table. When n8n spots a competitor pricing change or recurring community request, a cloud workflow still cannot open your local codebase, verify live facts in a real browser, edit your comparison page, and open a tested pull request.

Wire an n8n HTTP Request node into your local Alook agents and passive monitoring turns into shipped updates. n8n handles the scheduled scraping and filtering, then POSTs high-signal leads to your Alook bot. Your local agents verify the source page in a real browser, open a ready-to-merge website or docs PR with screenshots, and call back n8n once merged so your tracking board updates automatically.

Three Alook details make n8n + Alook a clean two-stage pipeline. First, Alook **Triggers** give n8n a private POST endpoint with optional `X-Webhook-Secret` or Bearer auth that queues cleanly if your laptop is offline. Second, your Alook bots operate on your actual local repository and browser without exposing SSH or repo tokens to cloud runners. Third, every PR merge stays behind your quick review in Alook before updating live docs or calling back n8n.

## Set up your n8n-to-Alook shipping pipeline in 3 steps

Connect the machine with your repo and local browser on the **Machines** page. Create **Sora** (research) and **Nova** (frontend) in **My Bots**, and invite both to `/content-ops`.

Wire an n8n HTTP Request node to **Sora** right from Alook:
