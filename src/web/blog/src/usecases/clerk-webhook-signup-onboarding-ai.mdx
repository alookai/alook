Your best B2B sign-ups quietly churn in their first ten minutes. An engineering manager creates a new Clerk Organization with a real company domain, hits one setup question, closes the tab, and never comes back. By the time you inspect your user table a week later, the window for a helpful personal note is long gone.

Connect Clerk webhooks to your growth agent in Alook and high-intent sign-ups turn into activated teams inside ten minutes. When `organization.created` or a work-email `user.created` fires, your bot filters out disposable sign-ups, checks the company's public stack, and drops a concise, stack-specific welcome draft into `/growth`. One tap sends the personal note, and your bot follows up if the team stalls before their first key milestone.

Three Alook mechanics keep outreach human instead of spammy. First, Alook **Triggers** verify Clerk's Standard Webhooks key privately on your growth bot so every free sign-up doesn't clutter Slack or email. Second, your bot surfaces only the two or three high-intent teams each day right inside your Alook `/growth` room. Third, every outbound note waits for your ten-second review before sending.

## Set up your Clerk sign-up activation loop in 3 steps

Connect your machine with your email CLI and product analytics script on the **Machines** page. Create **Sora** in **My Bots** and invite Sora to `/growth`.

Wire Clerk to **Sora** right from Alook:

    { label: "Endpoint URL", value: "https://alook.ai/api/webhooks/bot/trk_4vM7rK2pN8wQ5xL", mono: true },

  externalEvents={["organization.created", "user.created", "organizationMembership.created"]}
  externalSubmitLabel="Create"
  connectedSummary="Clerk Standard Webhooks secret saved. Ready to qualify and welcome high-intent teams."
/>

Instruct Sora in its workspace rules to skip free consumer email domains (`gmail.com`, `outlook.com`) unless a user invites multiple teammates, so `/growth` stays high-signal. Pair this with our [Resend inbound support loop](/usecases/resend-inbound-email-support-loop) so replies to your welcome notes feed straight back into the same team.
