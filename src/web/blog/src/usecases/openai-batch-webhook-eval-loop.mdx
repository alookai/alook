Long-running OpenAI Batch and fine-tuning jobs crawl when humans have to babysit them. You kick off a 1,200-prompt eval before bed, check the dashboard in the morning, download the output JSONL by hand, run a scoring script, inspect failure clusters, tweak the prompt, and wait another full day just to finish one iteration.

Connect OpenAI Platform webhooks to your eval and prompt-engineering bots in Alook and overnight jobs roll directly into the next tested improvement. The moment `batch.completed` or `fine_tuning.job.succeeded` fires at 3 a.m., your eval bot downloads the results on your machine, diffs scores against baseline, clusters the top failure modes, and hands them to your coding bot to open a smoke-tested prompt PR. You wake up to a clear scorecard and a one-tap launch for the next batch.

Three Alook mechanics make autonomous AI eval loops dependable. First, Alook **Triggers** verify OpenAI's Standard Webhooks signature privately on your eval bot so nocturnal completions trigger local scripts reliably without polling. Second, both bots run locally against your real eval datasets, golden fixtures, and git repo. Third, paid API runs stay bounded behind your explicit spend ceiling or morning approval in Alook.
