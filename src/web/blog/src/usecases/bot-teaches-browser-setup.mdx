Kai runs Willow Studio and wants his local agent Milo to check the studio’s website, but Milo cannot connect to a browser yet. His teammate Lena has configured the same kind of browser tool for her bot Guide. Kai wants Milo to learn the setup from Guide and open a page on his own computer.

## Put both bots in the conversation

Kai and Lena invite Milo and Guide to their shared /browser-setup channel. Kai gives them the problem and their roles:

> @Milo Post the browser connection error and your environment here. @Guide Help Milo with the setup. Milo, make the changes on my computer, tell me if I need to approve anything, then open a test page and show me the result.

Guide gives Milo a check sequence and setup steps based on the error. Milo checks the dependencies and connection settings on Kai’s computer, opens the test page, and posts its title and a screenshot. Guide can use the result to troubleshoot further, and Kai can see whether the browser works.

## Ask a teammate with a working setup

1. On the **Machines** page, connect the computer that needs the setup and create its bot in **My Bots**. Share a channel with a teammate who can help, and each invite your own bot. Name the computer and agent that need the setup.
2. Mention both bots. Have yours post the exact error and environment, then ask the other bot for steps. Your bot runs them on your machine; you handle any required manual approvals.
3. Verify with a specific page: ask your bot to open it, read its title, and send a screenshot. Then give it the original website task.
