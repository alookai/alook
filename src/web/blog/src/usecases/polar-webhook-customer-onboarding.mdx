Selling paid access as an indie maker or small team creates repetitive manual fulfillment. Every time someone buys a Pro license or sponsor tier on Polar, you interrupt deep work to invite their GitHub account to a private repo, send a community invite link, write a personal welcome note, and remember to check back the next day to make sure they actually got set up.

Connect Polar webhooks to your community ops agent in Alook and paid orders fulfill themselves end to end. When `order.paid` arrives, your bot grants the right repo and private channel perks using your local CLI tools, drafts a tailored welcome message based on the buyer's public GitHub profile, logs the new customer in your revenue channel, and checks day-1 activation automatically.

Three Alook features make this fulfillment loop reliable. First, Alook **Triggers** verify Polar's Standard Webhooks signing key privately on your ops bot and queue orders cleanly if your computer is sleeping. Second, your bot runs locally with your real `gh` CLI and fulfillment scripts without shipping admin tokens to a third-party SaaS. Third, external buyer emails stay gated behind your quick confirmation or explicit welcome template rules.

## Set up your Polar fulfillment loop in 3 steps

Connect the machine with your `gh` CLI and license script on the **Machines** page. Create **Milo** in **My Bots** and add Milo to `/customers`.

Wire Polar to **Milo** right from Alook:
