Cross-service incidents burn their first fifteen minutes just getting people in the same room. A Datadog SLO monitor trips, someone creates a war-room channel, and backend, frontend, and infra engineers all ask each other who shipped last. Once traffic stabilizes, somebody still has to reconstruct the timeline by hand for the postmortem.

Connect Datadog Monitor webhooks to a coordinator agent in Alook and your multi-agent war room assembles itself in two minutes. The instant Datadog fires an alert, your coordinator bot opens an incident thread and `@mentions` your backend and frontend bots to investigate their own repositories and deploys in parallel. They hand you one clear mitigation option to approve, watch the Datadog monitor flip back to **OK**, and write the clean postmortem automatically.

Three Alook mechanics make multi-agent incident response work without chaos. First, Alook **Triggers** wake your coordinator bot quietly with optional `X-Webhook-Secret` validation. Second, specialist bots live on the machines that hold each service's code and CLI tools, collaborating in one transparent Alook thread so you see every finding at a glance. Third, any rate-limit, feature-flag, or rollback action waits on your explicit human go-ahead.

## Set up your Datadog multi-agent war room in 3 steps

Connect the machines holding your service repos and ops CLI on the **Machines** page. Create **Vega** (coordinator) alongside your service bots (**Atlas**, **Nova**) in **My Bots**, and invite all three into `/incidents`.

Wire Datadog Webhooks to **Vega** right from Alook:
