Private GitLab pipelines turn tiny mistakes into half-day stalls. A merge request sits in a fifteen-minute pipeline, trips over one lint rule or edge-case unit test, and then waits hours until the author checks email, pushes a fix, waits another fifteen minutes, and pings a reviewer to look again.

Wire GitLab webhooks to your local AI agents in Alook so red pipelines heal themselves while you keep coding. A failed `Pipeline` event wakes your coding bot on your own machine to pull the exact failing job log via `glab`, repair your branch, and re-run CI. Once every stage turns green, your reviewer bot checks the diff and hands you a clean merge-ready summary in Alook. One tap merges the MR and closes the linked GitLab issue.

Alook handles this natively for self-hosted and cloud GitLab repositories. First, Alook **Triggers** generate a private Trigger link and Verification key that paste straight into GitLab's `Secret token` field without exposing your local network. Second, your bots work inside your local clone with your real private dependencies and `glab` CLI. Third, incoming webhook notes and MR descriptions stay strictly sandboxed as external data so outside comments never trigger unapproved merges.

## Set up your GitLab pipeline auto-fix loop in 3 steps

Connect the computer with your private GitLab clone and authenticated `glab` CLI on the **Machines** page. Create **Atlas** and **Iris** in **My Bots**, and invite both into `/core-mrs`.

Wire GitLab to **Atlas** right from Alook:
