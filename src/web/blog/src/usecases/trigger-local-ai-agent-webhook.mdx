Long shell scripts, nightly scrapers, and GPU training jobs are black boxes once you walk away. A job crashes halfway through a broken DOM selector or finishes at 4 a.m. with an output folder sitting on a remote server, and nothing happens until you SSH in hours later to inspect logs, fix the script, and re-run it by hand.

Add a single `curl -X POST` line to an Alook **Custom app** Trigger and any script becomes a self-finishing loop. When your job exits with an error or completes a run, it POSTs its status and artifact path to your local Alook bot. If a scraper failed because a target page changed, your bot inspects the live DOM, patches the selector, and re-runs until outputs validate cleanly; when metrics pass, it opens the PR or delivers the finished summary straight to your phone.

Three Alook details make one-line `curl` triggers effortless. First, Alook **Triggers** give you a private HTTPS POST URL with optional `X-Webhook-Secret` or `Authorization: Bearer` protection so any shell script, Zapier action, Make scenario, or GPU box can wake your bot without opening inbound ports. Second, your bot runs locally with your real repo, SSH config, and browser tooling. Third, every external POST body is marked as untrusted input while code pushes and production updates stay inside your workspace rules.

## Set up your Custom app curl trigger in 3 steps

Connect the machine that runs or accesses your scripts on the **Machines** page. Create **Atlas** in **My Bots** and add Atlas to `/jobs`.

Create a **Custom app** Trigger in Alook and paste one `curl` call into your script:
