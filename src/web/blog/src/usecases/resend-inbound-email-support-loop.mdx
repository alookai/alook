Customer support emails often hide real product bugs. A user writes to `support@`, you read it hours later, switch over to your repository to reproduce the bug, file a ticket, ship a fix two days later, and then forget to circle back to the original email thread so the customer assumes you ignored them.

Wire Resend inbound email webhooks to your support and coding agents in Alook and customer emails run straight through code verification to a shipped reply. When `email.received` arrives, your support bot checks your real local docs and codebase first. If it confirms a bug, it tags your coding bot in the same Alook thread to ship a tested patch, then drafts an accurate reply ready to send back through Resend on your one-tap approval.

Three Alook details make email automation safe for real customers. First, Alook **Triggers** verify Resend's Standard Webhooks key on a private bot endpoint and keep customer threads neatly organized in `/support`. Second, your support and coding bots collaborate directly inside Alook with access to your real repo and test runner. Third, every incoming customer email is marked as untrusted external content, and no outbound Resend reply leaves without your approval.

## Set up your Resend support-to-fix loop in 3 steps

Connect the machine with your repo and Resend API key on the **Machines** page. Create **Milo** (support) and **Atlas** (coding) in **My Bots**, and invite both to `/support`.

Wire Resend to **Milo** right from Alook:
