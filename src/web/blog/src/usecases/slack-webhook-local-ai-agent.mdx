Enterprise customers live in shared Slack Connect channels, while your real codebases and specialized AI agents live on your own machines in Alook. Every time a customer asks a deep technical question or reports an edge-case bug in Slack, you copy-paste context back and forth, wait on engineering to check the code, and manually craft a clean reply back in the Slack thread.

Connect Slack Event Subscriptions to your support agent in Alook and your external Slack channels get a private local engineering team behind them. When a customer tags your app or posts in `#vip-acme`, Slack wakes your support bot privately inside Alook. Your support and coding bots inspect your local repo, verify the exact answer or ship a fix, and let you approve a clean response that posts straight back into the original Slack thread without leaking a single line of private internal discussion.

Three Alook rules keep external Slack rooms safe. First, Alook **Triggers** verify Slack's Signing Secret on a private bot endpoint before waking your team. Second, all code inspection, internal bot handoffs, and PR fixes stay strictly inside your private Alook channel, never exposed in the customer's Slack workspace. Third, every outgoing Slack reply waits for your one-tap approval so nothing goes to a customer unreviewed.

## Set up your Slack-to-Alook support loop in 3 steps

Connect the machine with your repo and Slack Bot token on the **Machines** page. Create **Milo** (support) and **Atlas** (coding) in **My Bots**, and invite both to `/vip-support`.

Wire Slack to **Milo** right from Alook (paste Slack's Signing Secret into Alook first so Slack's automatic URL verification succeeds immediately):
