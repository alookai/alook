Edge 5xx alerts never wait for business hours. When a Cloudflare Worker starts throwing errors after a deploy, you usually scramble onto a laptop, open three Cloudflare tabs, correlate recent git commits, figure out the right rollback command, run it, and refresh the endpoint until traffic recovers.

Connect Cloudflare Notification webhooks to your on-call agent in Alook and that thirty-minute fire drill becomes a three-minute phone confirmation. The instant Cloudflare fires a Worker error-rate alert, your ops bot probes the failing route, diffs recent commits against the edge version, and brings in your coding bot to confirm whether a rollback or one-line hotfix is safer. You tap approve from your phone, your bot rolls back via `wrangler`, and keeps checking until the route returns clean `200 OK`.

Alook keeps high-stakes edge actions fast without giving up safety. First, Alook **Triggers** accept Cloudflare webhook notifications directly with optional secret verification so alerts wake only your ops bot. Second, your bot runs locally with your real git history and `wrangler` CLI, so diagnosis happens in seconds instead of copy-pasting dashboards. Third, destructive production rollbacks stay gated behind your explicit confirmation in Alook.

## Set up your Cloudflare edge-recovery loop in 3 steps

Connect the machine that has your repo and signed-in `wrangler` CLI on the **Machines** page. Create your ops bot (**Vega**) and coding bot (**Atlas**) in **My Bots**, and add both to `/edge-ops`.

Wire Cloudflare Notifications to **Vega** right from Alook:
