Frontend deploys break silently right after you switch tabs. You push a commit, close your laptop or jump into another task, and twenty minutes later a designer asks why the preview link is missing. By the time you dig through Vercel build logs, fix a stray type import, re-push, and grab desktop and mobile screenshots for review, an hour is gone.

Wire Vercel deployment webhooks into your local frontend agent in Alook and broken builds fix themselves before anyone notices. A `deployment.error` event wakes your coding bot to pull the failing build log, repair the PR branch locally, and push again. Once Vercel emits `deployment.ready`, your bot opens the real preview URL in a browser on your computer, verifies zero console errors, captures desktop and mobile screenshots, and drops them straight into your design review channel.

Three Alook mechanics keep this loop tight. First, Alook **Triggers** verify every Vercel webhook signature privately on your bot and queue events automatically if your computer is offline. Second, because your frontend agent runs on your own machine with your local repo and real browser tooling, it fixes code and inspects the live preview without brittle CI screenshot scripts. Third, every webhook payload arrives marked as untrusted external data so only your workspace rules decide which PR branches the bot can push to.

## Set up your Vercel auto-fix preview loop in 3 steps

Connect your dev machine with your frontend repository and local browser tool on the **Machines** page. Create your frontend bot (**Nova**) in **My Bots** and add it to `/design-review`.

Wire Vercel to **Nova** right from Alook:
