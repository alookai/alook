Failed renewals and card disputes leak revenue quietly when you are busy shipping product. A `payment_failed` email gets buried, an annual customer churns without anyone noticing, or a chargeback hits its evidence deadline while you are still hunting through login logs, invoices, and support threads to assemble a PDF.

Wire Stripe webhooks to your revenue agent in Alook and every failed payment or dispute runs a complete recovery loop. When `invoice.payment_failed` or `charge.dispute.created` fires, your revenue bot pulls account usage and billing history, checks with your support bot whether the customer hit any recent bugs, and puts a tailored recovery email or dispute evidence packet in front of you. You tap approve from your phone, your bot sends the outreach or files the evidence, and tracks Stripe until the money comes back.

Alook keeps sensitive billing actions fast and controlled. First, Alook **Triggers** verify Stripe's `whsec_` signature on a private bot endpoint so customer billing data stays in your private ops room. Second, your revenue and support bots collaborate in one Alook thread with access to your real product analytics and support logs. Third, no customer email or Stripe dispute submission goes out without your explicit confirmation.

## Set up your Stripe revenue-recovery loop in 3 steps

Connect your machine with Stripe CLI and email/CRM credentials on the **Machines** page. Create **Clara** (revenue) and **Milo** (support) in **My Bots**, and add both to `/revenue-ops`.

Wire Stripe to **Clara** right from Alook:

  botName="Clara"

  sampleSecret="whsec_str_9c4e2a7f8b1d0653"

  externalFields={[
    { label: "Endpoint URL", value: "https://alook.ai/api/webhooks/bot/trk_2fR8mN5vK9pL3xC", mono: true },

  externalEvents={["invoice.payment_failed", "charge.dispute.created", "invoice.paid"]}
  externalSubmitLabel="Create destination"
  connectedSummary="Stripe signing secret saved. Ready to triage failed payments and disputes."
/>

Run the same pattern for `charge.dispute.created` so Clara gathers login timestamps, invoice receipts, and support history into a ready-to-submit evidence bundle well before Stripe's deadline.
